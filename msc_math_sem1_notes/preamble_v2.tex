\chapter*{How to use this booklet}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{How to use this booklet}

\begin{intuitbox}
This booklet is your \emph{single source of truth} for MSc Mathematics
Semester 1 at Manipal University Jaipur, and a focused launch pad for
the CSIR NET (Mathematical Sciences) examination. Every chapter is
written in three layers: (i) a friendly \textbf{Quick Refresher} that
re-grounds you in the prerequisites, (ii) the formal \textbf{theory},
written exactly as it must appear in your university answer scripts,
and (iii) \textbf{worked examples and CSIR NET-style questions} so you
build pattern recognition.
\end{intuitbox}

\textbf{Reading order.} For a first pass, read each chapter linearly.
Skip nothing in the prerequisites chapter -- it is the foundation on
which everything else stands. For revision, jump to the
\emph{CSIR NET Quick Revision} chapter and the \emph{Hit-Rate Priority
Analysis} to plan your study sprints.

\textbf{Color code.}\\[2pt]
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.32\textwidth}p{0.62\textwidth}@{}}
\textcolor{DefBlue}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Definition (blue)} & A formal definition. Memorise verbatim.\\
\textcolor{ThmPlum}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Theorem (plum)}    & A named result you must be able to state and prove.\\
\textcolor{LemmaTeal}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Lemma / Corollary} & Supporting result.\\
\textcolor{ProofGray}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Proof (gray)}    & The proof, written in exam style.\\
\textcolor{ExGreen}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Worked Example}    & A solved problem.\\
\textcolor{ExamRed}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{CSIR NET / Exam Tip} & A pattern that the exam loves.\\
\textcolor{TipAmber}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Memory Aid}        & A mnemonic.\\
\textcolor{IntuitTeal}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Plain-English Intuition} & The picture behind the symbols.\\
\textcolor{PreReqSand}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Quick Refresher} & Class-12 / earlier-semester recap.\\
\textcolor{WarnOrange}{\rule{1.2em}{0.7em}}\;\textbf{Common Pitfall}  & A trap that loses students marks.\\
\end{tabular}

\textbf{Use a pen.} Mathematics is not a spectator sport. As you read,
\emph{re-derive} every equation -- this is non-negotiable. The proofs
are written so you can copy them verbatim into your university scripts;
do not memorise them, \emph{master} them.
