## 🏆 Exam Strategy

### For Manipal Semester Exams
- Focus on **all units equally** — university exams test breadth
- Practice **worked examples** from each unit — exam questions are variations
- Memorize **key theorems and their statements** — they ask "State and prove"

### For CSIR NET
- Focus on **problem-solving speed** — it's MCQ-based
- Practice **previous year questions** extensively
- Build **conceptual clarity** over rote memorization
- Master **Linear Algebra + Real Analysis** first — they carry ~40% weightage combined
